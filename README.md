# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Data Analysis & Sales Dashboard (2022)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive exploratory data analysis and interactive sales performance dashboard for **Vrinda Store** (an e-commerce apparel business) for the year 2022. The goal is to analyze customer demographics, order status breakdown, top-performing sales channels, and geographic distribution to drive business growth and targeted marketing strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Handled missing values, standardized age groups (Adult, Senior, Teenager), and derived order metrics.
- **Data Summarization:** Excel Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts.
- **Dashboard Features:** Slicers (Month, Channel, Category) for interactive business reporting.

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights & Findings

1. **Target Audience Demographics:** 
   - **Women** represent the primary buyers (~65%+ of overall orders and revenue).
   - The **Adult group (age 30-49)** contributes the highest revenue share.
2. **Top Sales Channels:** 
   - **Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart** emerge as the top 3 revenue-generating channels.
3. **Geographic Concentration:** 
   - Top contributing states include **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**.
4. **Order Status:** 
   - Over **90%+ orders** were successfully delivered, with minor cancellations/refunds.
5. **Product Category Preference:** 
   - **Sets and Kurtas** are the top-selling categories in terms of total order quantity and sales amount.

---

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations
- Target female adult consumers (ages 30–49) across Maharashtra, Karnataka, and UP with personalized marketing campaigns on Instagram/Meta ads.
- Strengthen inventory allocation for **Kurtas and Sets** during peak sales periods.
- Focus promotional ads primarily on Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart platforms to maximize ROI.
